FAQ

QUICK ANSWERS BEFORE YOU BOOK

How do I book?

Go to Book Now, tap an open date, and fill out the booking form. Then pay the 50% downpayment using our QR code and send us your payment screenshot. We'll email your booking confirmation once it's verified.

How much is the downpayment?

50% of your total. Your date is officially reserved once we receive it.

What's the difference between Classic and Premium?

Classic uses standard thermal paper and covers up to 150 guests. Premium covers up to 200 guests, uses high-quality waterproof sticker paper, comes with 2 attendants, and includes 30 free magnetic prints. Check Rates for the full list.

How many photos can we print?

Unlimited sessions during your booked hours, with up to 6 prints per session.

How long will the booth stay at our event?

The whole event, up to 5 hours, plus prep time for setup. Need more time? Add extra hours when you book.

Can you match our theme?

Yes! Every layout is customized for your event. Tell us your theme and the celebrant's name in the booking form, and send us photos on Messenger so we can design it.

Do you go outside Iligan City?

Yes, a transport fee applies for venues outside Iligan City. Tick the box in the booking form and we'll confirm the fee with you.

What do you need at the venue?

A small space for the booth and access to a power outlet. Message us your venue details and we'll confirm the setup with you.

What's the self-serve option?

Classic self-serve has no attendant staying at the booth means you will operate the booth yourself, but we're on call within 15 minutes if you need us.

How do we take care of the prints?

Keep thermal prints away from heat and direct sunlight. The free brown envelope helps protect them. For keepsakes that last longer, go for Premium's sticker paper or add magnetic prints.

Can I change my date?

Message us as early as possible and we'll check which dates are still open for you.
